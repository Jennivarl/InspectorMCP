How it works

Look. Decide. Look closer. Act.

Every step is an OpenCV 5 tool exposed over MCP. What the agent sees decides what it calls next.

perceivedecidelook closeract
01

Look

Straighten the photo, split it into cells, check image quality. split_panel · check_quality

02

Score

CellNet rates each cell 0–1 in OpenCV's DNN; classic OpenCV marks cracks and dark areas. scan_cell

03

Second look

Unsure? Re-score from mirrored, flipped and enhanced views. second_look

04

Locate

Hide each patch in turn — the biggest score drop is where the defect is. locate_defect

05

Zoom

Measure the mark up close: length, straightness, contrast. inspect_region

06

Act

Pass it, open a repair ticket, or hand it to a human. mark_pass · open_ticket · request_approval
